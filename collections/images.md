# Images

Image galleries and tools for finding backgrounds, illustrations, and other visuals for projects.

[Back to the collection](../README.md)

## [posts.design](https://posts.design/)

A searchable gallery of social media posts from brands, with collections of headers and Open Graph images. Browse examples by post type, such as launches, announcements, and event promotions.

- **Tags:** images, inspiration, social-media, branding, headers, og-images

## [Imageory](https://imageory.in/)

A personal gallery of AI-generated images, such as landscapes, abstract art, and pixel backgrounds, each shown with the prompt used to create it. Browse by category or search by color, then copy a prompt to make your own variations.

- **Tags:** images, ai, prompts, backgrounds, illustrations

## [Lummi](https://www.lummi.ai/)

A library of royalty-free AI-generated stock photos, illustrations, and 3D renders, made by digital artists. Browse or search for visuals to use in projects, and remove image backgrounds for free.

- **Tags:** images, ai, stock-photos, illustrations, 3d

## [Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so/)

A visual inspiration platform for saving images into collections, similar to a moodboard. Search by color, find similar images from one you like, save from the web with the browser extension, and collaborate on collections with others. Settings let you show, blur, or hide images flagged as AI-generated.

- **Tags:** images, moodboards, collections, color, inspiration

## [Aura](https://auragradients.vercel.app/)

A free, open-source library of ambient gradient backgrounds built from layered CSS blend modes. Filter by styles such as mesh, nebula, prism, grain, and glass, then preview a background and copy it or its prompt.

- **Tags:** images, backgrounds, gradients, css, open-source

## [Backgrounds Supply](https://www.backgrounds.supply/)

A library of handcrafted and AI-generated backgrounds, wallpapers, and gradients in collections such as smudge, ASCII, portals, and animated backgrounds, with new drops added monthly. Most of the library requires a one-time purchase that allows commercial use; a freebies page offers some free backgrounds.

- **Tags:** images, backgrounds, gradients, wallpapers, animation

## [gradients.sh](https://gradients.sh/)

A library of animated gradient backgrounds and UI pieces for React, rendered with WebGPU or WebGL2 and themed through your CSS variables. Free components, such as mesh, aurora, grain, and gradient text, are MIT licensed and install as source code with the shadcn CLI; Pro effects and most of the presets require a paid account. Backgrounds can also be exported as PNG or MP4, or added to plain HTML pages with an embed script.

- **Tags:** images, backgrounds, gradients, animation, react
